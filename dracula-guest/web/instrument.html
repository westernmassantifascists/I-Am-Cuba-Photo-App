Play with the mouse or a finger, or the computer keyboard: the bottom two rows (Z S X D C …) are the lower octave, the top two rows (Q 2 W 3 E …) the one above. Shift or the arrow keys move the octave. Hold Space for the sustain pedal. Strings, brass and organ hold as long as you hold the key; harp, spiccato and drums ring out. Pressing a second instrument while holding Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) layers it with the first.

Instruments: VSCO 2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios (CC0); organ sampled by Simon Dalzell. Wolves: US Fish & Wildlife Service (public domain). Thunder: Bidgee (CC BY 3.0) and Caesar (public domain), via Wikimedia Commons.
